This is a sample post. Everything you see here was written in the admin panel and saved to your GitHub repository, which is how the site stays fast and easy for search engines to read.

## How a post gets published

Open `/admin/`, sign in, and choose **New post**. Write in the editor, fill in the search title and description, then press **Publish**. Your site rebuilds and the post goes live in about a minute.

## Where your leads go

Scroll down: every post ends with a lead form. Each submission is saved in the **Leads** tab of the admin panel and, if you set it up, emailed to you straight away.

## What to do next

1. Delete this post and the other sample post.
2. Open **Settings** and change the site name, tagline and form wording.
3. Write a post that answers one question your customers really ask.
