A post ranks when it answers the exact question someone typed into a search box, better than the pages already there. Everything else is detail.

## Pick one question

Write the question as a customer would ask it. "How much does a conference AV setup cost?" beats "Our AV services". One post, one question.

## Write the title and description for people

The **search title** appears in Google results. Keep it under 60 characters and put the question's key words first. The **description** is your pitch under the title: under 160 characters, with a reason to click.

## Answer early, then go deeper

Give the short answer in the first paragraph. Use headings that read like questions, because readers and search engines scan them.

### Add proof

A price range, a photo from a real job, or a number from your own work makes a post harder to copy.

## End with one next step

The lead form under every post is the next step. Mention it in your last paragraph so the reader knows what happens if they fill it in.
